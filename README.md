# Best Tree Friends

A talking tree growing game. Take a quiz to find your tree, care for it, and chat with it.

Play it at https://jsholmes.github.io/best-tree-friends/

Play it at https://jsholmes.github.io/best-tree-friends/

On an iPad, open the link in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen" to get a Best Tree Friends icon.
Everything you grow is saved only on the device you play on.
